Install on the device you write on.

Native apps for Mac and Windows, plus a Chrome zip until the extension is on the Chrome Web Store.

macOS menu bar

Select text in Mail, Slack, or a browser, press the shortcut, then Replace or copy the result back.

Download for Mac

macOS 13 or later. Grant Accessibility when asked. If Gatekeeper blocks the first open, right-click the app and choose Open.

Windows tray

Select text in any app, press Ctrl Shift E, then Replace to put the result back, or copy from the tray panel.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or later. SmartScreen may warn on unsigned builds: More info, then Run anyway.

Chrome extension

Works on any site from the toolbar, the context menu, or Ctrl/Cmd Shift E. Replace puts the result back into the field.

Download zip
  1. 1Unzip the file.
  2. 2Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. 3Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder (the one with manifest.json).

The same panel on every device.

Rewrite

Change tone, tighten a draft, or translate. Three variants plus a word-level diff of what changed.

Format

Turn notes into Markdown, HTML, bullets, a table, an outline, a summary, or an FAQ.

Reply

Paste a received message, add a few notes, and draft a chat or email reply in the length and language you want.

Writing assist

Tab autocomplete as you type, plus Check writing for grammar and wording.

Voice profile

Paste a few samples of how you write. Rewrite, Reply, and writing assist match that voice. Stays on the device.

Replace

Copy the result, or Replace to put it back into the field or app you came from.

Bring your own API key.

There is no Rewrite Better account. Paste a Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, or OpenAI key in Settings. Keys are tried in that order when one hits quota. Requests go from your device to the provider.

Open Groq console

Your key stays on your device.

This site does not receive your text or API key. The apps talk to your AI provider directly. Read the full note on the privacy page.
