Three jobs. One panel.

Rewrite

Change tone, tighten a draft, or translate. Friendly, professional, concise, persuasive, or casual.

Format

Turn notes into Markdown, HTML, bullets, a table, an outline, a summary, or an FAQ.

Reply

Paste a received message, add a few notes, and draft a chat or email reply in the length and language you want.

Bring your own Groq key.

There is no Rewrite Better account. Create a free key at console.groq.com, paste it in Settings, and requests go from your device to Groq.

Open Groq console

Your key stays on your device.

This site does not receive your text or API key. The apps talk to Groq directly. Read the full note on the privacy page.
